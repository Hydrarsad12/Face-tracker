Why mobile camera might fail:

  • Insecure Context: On mobile phones, Chrome and Safari block the camera unless accessed via https:// or localhost.
  • Chrome Flag: When accessing http://192.168.x.x:8080 on Android, visit chrome://flags/#unsafely-treat-insecure-origin-as-secure and add your IP.
  • Permissions: Check if camera access was blocked in browser site settings.
